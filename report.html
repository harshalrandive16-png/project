📋 Reporting Guidelines

  • Stay Safe: Do not approach an active hazard (flood water/landslide) to take photos. Stay at a safe distance.
  • Be Accurate: Ensure your GPS location is turned on for accurate GIS mapping.
  • Clear Media: Try to capture the scale of the blockage and any nearby landmarks for AI analysis.
  • Verify Once: Submit only one report per incident to avoid duplication.
